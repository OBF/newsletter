# OBF Newsletter November 2026

TL;DR: Recapping BOSC and CoFest, changes to the OBF Board, 10 years of event fellowships and community news.

In longer form: 

## OBF News

### The OBF Board Meeting 

Mid-September, the OBF held [its public board meeting](https://www.open-bio.org/2026/09/04/obf-public-board-meeting-sept-2026/). During the event, we thanked our outgoing members Iddo Friedberg and Hilyatuz Zahroh for their service and re-elected the members who stood for re-election.

### OBF Event Fellowships

Our event fellowship [turned 10 this year](https://www.open-bio.org/2026/11/06/2026-11-06-10-years-of-Event-Award/) and during this time we supported 70 fellows from traditionally underrepresented groups in attending events around the world.
Originally launched purely as a travel fellowship for in-person attendance, we later included remote/virtual participation to offer more participants the possibility to join events.

From this round onward, we are changing our fellowship review cadence to two times a year, with deadlines in April and October. Our next round of applications will close April 1, 2027 (in time for BOSC).

To continue running the fellowship in the long term, we rely on volunteer labor and funding. So if you can spare some time to help out or donate to help fund the program, please get in touch with the OBF.

### BOSC news
 
BOSC 2026 took place on July 14-15 in Washington DC, USA, featuring [two keynote talks, two panels, and a range of of submitted talks and posters](https://www.open-bio.org/events/bosc-2026/bosc-2026-schedule/).
A blog post about the event can be found at https://www.open-bio.org/2026/08/16/2026-08-16-BOSC2026-recap/. A report will be published soon in [F1000Research](https://f1000research.com/collections/BOSC).

BOSC was followed by the [CollaborationFest](https://www.open-bio.org/events/bosc-2026/collaborationfest/) on July 17-18, which drew 30 participants collaborating on 7 projects. A report about CoFest 2026 will appear soon in F1000Research.

Next year, the 28th edition of [BOSC](https://open-bio.org/events/bosc) will take place two days between July 18-22 in Copenhagen, Denmark, as part of ISMB/ECCB 2027.

## Community updates

### OBF Member Project working on AI policies.

Both BioPerl and Biopython are still actively discussing their stance on AI-assisted contributions,
but there is agreement that communications should be human (allowing for machine translation
to English). BioRuby and BioJava have already taken a permissive stance.

You can read more about the ongoing discussions in BioPerl and Biopython in these issues:

* https://github.com/bioperl/bioperl-live/issues/407
* https://github.com/biopython/biopython/pull/5241
* https://github.com/biopython/biopython/pull/5388
